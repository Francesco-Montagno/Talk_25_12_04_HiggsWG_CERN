# EFT Theory Uncertainties at the LHC - CERN 22nd Higgs WG Workshop Slides - 04/12/2025

These are the slides from the talk I gave at CERN for the Higgs WG Workshop.  

They are based on our paper [arXiv:2507.15954](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.15954), written by [S.Chang, M.Luty, T.Ma, A.Wulzer]() and [myself]().

All slides were generated using the **Manim** package.

If you couldn’t attend or want to know more, feel free to reach out to me by email [fmontagno@ifae.es]()—I’m happy to answer questions!
